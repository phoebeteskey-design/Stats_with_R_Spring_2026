Results:

We found a significant interaction between Temperature and Species (p < 0.001), indicating that mass is dependent on both temperature and species. The coefficient of Temp.C (-0.013) indicates that as temperature increases, there is a slight decrease in mass. There are large differences between the baseline mass of the reference species (AB = e^1.788 = 5.977) and the other beetle species. The fixed effects of Temperature and Species account for 99.4% of variability in mass. Including the random effect Sex, the model accounts for 99.7% of variability in mass.
